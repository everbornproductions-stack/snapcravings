A few tips to get better recipes out of SnapCravings:

- Open the fridge door wide and snap in good light. Shelves you can see are ingredients it can use.
- Add a craving ("something spicy", "quick pasta") to steer the recipes.
- Set your allergies and diet once in Settings. They're treated as hard rules on every recipe.
- Tap "More ideas" if the first batch isn't your thing.
