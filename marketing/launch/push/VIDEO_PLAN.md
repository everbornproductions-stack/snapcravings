# SnapCravings short-video plan: 30 scripts, 4-week calendar, hashtags
Formats: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts. 9:16, 1080x1920, 15-30 s, captions burned in (most people watch muted), hook in the first 2 seconds, app shown on a real phone screen in the existing app style (no redesign). End card: "SnapCravings, free on Google Play" + "An Everborn Production".

**Who makes them:** Chris's **Everborn Reels**, **Capital City Reels** and **Music Video Studio** agents can produce these from this file (stills + screen captures from the live app: cap-reel*.mjs / rec-reel3.mjs in the repo already record the app). Music Video Studio can make the short original jingles/beds so no licensed music is needed. **Posting needs Chris's yes.** Before launch, the CTA says "link in bio" and the bio points to https://snapcravings.pages.dev; on launch day swap to the Play link (or a custom store listing URL for attribution).

Rules for every video: no fake reviews or "5 stars" asks; no "#1/best app" claims; savings numbers must come from a real Crave it result recorded that day (prices vary); say "AI estimate" when showing calories; allergy videos say "always double-check labels".
