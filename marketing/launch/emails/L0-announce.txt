SnapCravings is now on Google Play.

Snap a photo of your fridge and get recipes you can cook right now, matched to what you're craving. It follows your allergies and diet as hard rules, works in 10 languages (English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Indonesian, French, German, Arabic, Russian and Turkish), and shows whether cooking beats ordering delivery, fees included. Recipes stream in one by one, and around the holidays there's a Holiday foods button for classic dishes.

Get it on Google Play:

On iPhone? Open https://snapcravings.pages.dev in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The App Store version is coming.
