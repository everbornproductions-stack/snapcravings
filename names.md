# App name ideas — DRAFT (2026-10-20)

*an Everborn product · "Make it or buy it." is the working tagline*

**How these were checked:** one web search per name on 2026-10-20 (`"<name>" app`). This is a quick conflict scan, **not** a trademark search. Before choosing, run a USPTO trademark search plus App Store, Google Play and domain checks.

| # | Name | Rationale | Existing app/brand check |
|---|------|-----------|--------------------------|
| 1 | **Make or Take** ⭐ TOP 3 | Rhymes, and says the whole product in three words: make it at home or take it out. | Checked: no app found under this exact name. Closest is "MakeThis", a swipe-style recipe picker (Product Hunt). |
| 2 | **Crave Math** ⭐ TOP 3 | A craving plus the math on it. Signals "we do the fee math for you". | Checked: no exact match. A similar "Crave for Math" flashcard app exists (APKPure, last update 2018), and Crave Creative makes math games. Low conflict, different category. |
| 3 | **Pantry or Pickup** ⭐ TOP 3 | Names the two choices directly, and the alliteration makes it easy to say. | Checked: no exact match. "Pantry Plus" (campus food-pantry pickup) and several pantry-organizer apps exist. "Pantry" is a crowded word, so this name is descriptive but harder to trademark. |
| 4 | Stove or Store | Short, rhyming choice between cooking and buying. | Checked: no exact match, but "Stove" is an existing shoppable recipe and grocery app (Product Hunt), and STOVE is a gaming platform. Higher confusion risk. |
| 5 | Fridge or Fees | Points at the hidden-fee problem, which is the core differentiator. | Checked: no exact match. Smart-fridge apps such as Farmer's Fridge use "Fridge". Sounds a bit negative. |
| 6 | Crave Check | "Check" works two ways: check your craving, and the restaurant check. | Checked: no exact match, but the "Crave___" space is busy with craving-control and diet apps (CraveShift, Cravewatch, CraveLab, CraveCatch). Risk that people read it as a diet app. |
| 7 | SnapCrave | Combines the two home-screen actions, "Snap it" and "Crave it". | Checked: no exact match. "Snap" sits close to Snapchat/Snap Inc., which is a likely trademark headache. Several "Crave" food apps exist. |
| 8 | Hunger Math | Plain-spoken take on "do the math on dinner". | Checked: no exact match. Kids' math apps use "Hungry" (Motion Math: Hungry Guppy). "Hunger" also reads as food insecurity, so the tone is off. |
| 9 | Cook Call | Making the call: cook it or order it. | Checked: no exact match. Cooklang's "Cook" app and "CookConnect" exist. It sounds like a phone or video-call app. |
| 10 | Dish Decide | Clear decision-helper framing. | Checked: no exact match. Similar decider apps exist: Dishd, Dish.fm (2013) and Dinner Decider. Generic. |

## Top 3
1. **Make or Take**: most memorable, and it explains the product.
2. **Crave Math**: owns the money angle, the part competitors don't have.
3. **Pantry or Pickup**: the most literal option; good for app-store search, weaker for trademark.

## Logo / brand directions
1. **"The Fork in the Road"**: a single-line mark in which a fork's tines split into two paths, one ending in a pan (make) and one in a bag (buy). Warm cream background, coral (#E8613A) and deep espresso (#1E1B18). Friendly rounded sans for the wordmark, with "an Everborn product" in small gold italic underneath. This is the look used in the prototype.
2. **"The Receipt"**: the logo is a torn receipt whose total line is circled, or a checkmark. It leans into the hidden-fees story: honest, a bit cheeky, money-savvy. Off-white paper texture, a monospace accent font for numbers, and fresh green (#2F8F5B) for "you saved". It works well in social posts that show "the real total" breakdowns.
