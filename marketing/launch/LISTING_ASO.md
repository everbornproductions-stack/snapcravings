## Keyword research (from competitor listings, Nov 2026)
Competitors ranking for photo-to-recipe: RecipeGenie ("AI Recipe Finder"), "AI Recipe Creator from Camera", "AI Photo Cooking Recipes", SuperCook ("Recipe Generator"). Terms they all repeat:
- Primary: **recipe from photo**, **AI recipe**, **recipes from ingredients**, **what to cook**, **fridge**
- Secondary: recipe generator, recipe finder, pantry, leftovers, dinner ideas, meal ideas, food waste, shopping list, grocery list
- Diet/allergy (long tail, our differentiator with allergy filters): keto, vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, low-carb, high-protein, allergy
- Unique angle nobody owns: **cook vs. delivery cost** ("cheaper than delivery", "craving") – SuperCook/RecipeGenie don't mention it.
Rules applied: primary term in title + early in short & long desc; each key term 2-4x in the long description in natural sentences (Google metadata policy forbids stuffing, "best/#1", emoji spam).

## Full description (~2,500 chars, under 4,000)
Not sure what to cook? Snap a photo of your fridge or pantry and SnapCravings uses AI to spot your ingredients and turn them into recipes you can make tonight. It's the fastest way to get a recipe from a photo, use up leftovers, and skip another pricey delivery order.

SNAP YOUR FRIDGE, GET RECIPES
Take a few photos of your fridge, pantry, and spice shelf. SnapCravings recognizes your ingredients, sauces, and spices, then suggests recipes from the ingredients you already have, with clear step-by-step instructions.

RECIPES FOR YOUR DIET AND ALLERGIES
Set your diet once and every recipe fits: vegetarian, vegan, keto, low-carb, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, high-protein and more. Ingredients that break your diet or trigger an allergy are flagged, so dinner ideas stay safe for you and your family.

CRAVE IT: COOK OR ORDER?
Craving chicken pad thai or a burger? Name it and SnapCravings shows whether cooking it at home or ordering delivery is the better deal, including fees and tip. Most nights, cooking wins.

SHOPPING LIST IN ONE TAP
Missing an ingredient or running low? Add it to your grocery list in one tap.

SAVE YOUR FAVORITE RECIPES
Heart the meals you love and come back anytime.

LESS FOOD WASTE, LESS GUESSING
Stop asking "what should I make for dinner?" Use what's in your fridge before it goes bad, save money, and get quick meal ideas in under a minute.

MEAL PREP FOR THE WEEK
Plan a week of meals with one grocery list and a prep-day guide, with optional calorie and protein targets.

RECIPES IN SECONDS
Recipes stream in one by one, and a growing recipe library gives instant matches for common ingredients.

HOLIDAY MODE
Around the holidays in your country, the app dresses up in holiday colors with a short animation and a Holiday foods button for classic dishes. The Holiday planner builds a full holiday menu for your guest count, a scaled shopping list and a cooking timeline (full plan with Pro).
