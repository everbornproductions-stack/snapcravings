### Description (paste)
SnapCravings turns a photo of your fridge into recipes for what you are craving, fitted to your diet and allergies. Allergies are treated as hard rules: a recipe that breaks one is never shown. "Cook it or order it" compares a dish made at home with the delivery price, fees and tip included. A weekly meal-prep planner with a fitness and macros mode builds a week of meals and one grocery list. Plus a recipe library with ratings, seasonal collections and a place for your family recipes, adapted to your diet. Holiday mode adds Holiday foods for your country's holidays. In 10 languages, including full Spanish. Pro: $3.99/month founders price, then $4.99, or $39.99/year. Built by Everborn, a small independent studio.

### Helpful details (paste)
Accessibility: large tap targets, high-contrast text, works with Dynamic Type through the system web view (verify before submitting). Inclusivity: 10 languages (full Spanish, right-to-left Arabic, Hindi and more), allergy-first design for families managing food allergies. Priority: high; January meal prep is our main seasonal moment.
