SnapCravings Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

SnapCravings (the website at snapcravings.pages.dev and the Android app app.snapcravings.twa) is made by Everborn Marketing LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. SnapCravings is meant for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children.

What we collect

  • Photos you upload. When you snap your fridge or pantry, the photo is sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI service only to recognize ingredients and write recipes. We do not keep your photos after the request is answered and we do not use them for advertising.
  • Text you type. Cravings, diet and allergy settings, and meal-plan requests are processed the same way to make recipes. Diet settings, favorites and your shopping list are stored on your device.
  • Email address and purchase info. If you subscribe to SnapCravings Pro, your email address and subscription status (plan, purchase token or payment reference, renewal state) are used to unlock and restore Pro. Payments are handled by Google Play or Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic usage events. Simple, anonymous counts such as "scan" or "recipes shown" with a hashed random device id, to understand which features are used and to limit free scans. No cookies for tracking, no advertising IDs, no IP address stored.
  • Recipe ratings and saved recipes you choose to share, linked to the anonymous device id.

How we use it

Only to run the app: make recipes, enforce free-scan limits, provide and restore your subscription, prevent abuse, and fix problems. We do not sell your data and we show no ads.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run the app for us: Google (Gemini AI and Google Play Billing), Stripe (web payments), Supabase (database) and Cloudflare (hosting). They may process data only to provide their service. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Security and retention

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Photos are not stored. Subscription records are kept while your subscription is active and as long as needed for tax and legal records. Anonymous usage events are deleted or aggregated over time.

Your choices and deleting your data

You can clear on-device data at any time by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling. To ask us to delete data linked to your email (subscription records, ratings), email everbornproductions@gmail.com with the subject "SnapCravings data deletion". We reply within 30 days. Cancel a subscription in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or through the Stripe link in your receipt.

Children

SnapCravings is not for children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will delete the data.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this page with a new date. Questions: everbornproductions@gmail.com, Everborn Marketing LLC.
