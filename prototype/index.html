Chicken pad thai

30 minTotal
2Servings
EasyLevel

Steps · Let's get cooking!

  1. Soak the rice noodles in hot tap water for 8–10 minutes until bendy but still firm. Drain.
  2. Whisk the sauce: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp brown sugar, 1 tbsp peanut butter, juice of 1 lime and 2 tbsp warm water.
  3. Slice the chicken thin. Mince the garlic. Cut the green onions into 2-inch pieces.
  4. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan over high heat. Cook the chicken 4–5 minutes until browned and cooked through. Push it to one side.
  5. Add the garlic for 30 seconds, then crack in the eggs and scramble until just set.
  6. Add the noodles and sauce. Toss 2–3 minutes until the noodles soak up the sauce.
  7. Fold in the bean sprouts and green onions for 30 seconds. Serve with lime wedges.
